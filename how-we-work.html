What each stage means

A structured approach without unnecessary complexity.

01 / A

Align

Understand before changing.

We get close to the business, its people, commercial priorities and current sales environment. Together with leadership, we identify where performance is being constrained and align around what needs to change, what success looks like and how SaleSycle will support it.

02 / I

Integrate

Build the system around the business.

The Growth Engine is integrated into existing leadership, management and sales rhythms. Expectations, language and measures become consistent so everyone understands both the standard and their role within it.

03 / D

Deploy

Turn methodology into behaviour.

Training moves into application. Salespeople use the approach in live opportunities, managers coach against it and new behaviours become visible in everyday commercial activity.

04 / E

Elevate

Make improvement sustainable.

Performance is measured, reinforced and refined. Managers become increasingly self sufficient, stronger behaviours become habitual and the business develops a more predictable commercial rhythm.
